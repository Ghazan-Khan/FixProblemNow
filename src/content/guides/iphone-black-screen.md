## Rule out charge and power state

Connect the iPhone to a known-good charger and cable. Leave it for at least 20 minutes, even if the screen stays black. A completely flat iPhone may not show a charging icon immediately. Check the cable and port for lint, and try a different charger if you have one.

While it charges, listen for a vibration or a notification sound. If the iPhone responds, it is running and the display is the likely problem.

## Force restart the correct model

The button sequence depends on the model. Use the wrong one and nothing will happen.

- **iPhone 8 and later, including Face ID models:** press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then press and hold the side button until the Apple logo appears. This can take 10–20 seconds.
- **iPhone 7:** press and hold volume down and the side button until the logo appears.
- **iPhone 6s and earlier:** press and hold the Home button and the top or side button until the logo appears.

Do not slide to power off first; a force restart is a different action and does not erase data.

## Check whether the phone is alive

After the force restart, look for:

- A vibration when you move the mute switch, if your model has one.
- A ring when you call the iPhone from another phone.
- A charging sound when you connect the cable.
- The iPhone appearing in Finder or Apple Devices on a computer.

If any of these happen but the screen stays black, the display path is the likely fault. If none happen and the iPhone is not detected, the phone may not be starting at all.

## Use recovery mode carefully

If a computer detects the iPhone but the screen stays black, or if the iPhone is stuck on a black screen with a spinning circle, recovery mode may help.

1. Connect the iPhone to a computer with Finder or Apple Devices open.
2. Perform the force restart sequence for your model, but keep holding the side button until the recovery screen appears.
3. In Finder or Apple Devices, choose **Update** first. This attempts to reinstall iOS without erasing data.
4. Choose **Restore** only if Update fails and you have a backup, because Restore erases the iPhone.

If the computer never detects the iPhone, recovery mode is not available and the problem is likely hardware.

## If the screen stays black

- **Check with a torch.** A faint image at an angle suggests the backlight has failed.
- **Try a different cable and computer port** to rule out a charge-only cable or a faulty connection.
- **Look for a repair programme.** Search your iPhone model on Apple’s support site for a documented display issue.
- **Book a display inspection.** Ask whether data will be preserved and get a quote before work starts.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not restore or erase an iPhone that holds data you have not backed up unless Apple support advises it. A restore can make photos and messages unrecoverable if no backup exists.

## Confirm the fix

Once the screen returns, make a call, test the touchscreen across all edges, and check the front camera and sensors. Restart normally and confirm the display returns. If the screen fails again after a drop, heat, or pressure, tell the repairer; that pattern points to a hardware connection rather than software.
