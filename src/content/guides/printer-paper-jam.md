## Clear the jam in the right order

### 1. Turn off and unplug the printer

This protects you from moving parts and lets a laser printer cool. If the printer has a display, note the jam location it reports before switching off.

### 2. Open the access covers

Use the manual to find the tray, rear, and top access points for your model. Open one at a time and look for the sheet. A torch helps in darker areas, but do not put your hand near the fuser.

### 3. Pull slowly in the paper path direction

Grip the sheet with both hands and pull gently in the direction the paper normally moves. If it will not move, look for a release lever or roller clutch. Do not yank, and do not pull backwards against the rollers.

### 4. Look for torn pieces

A small scrap left behind causes the next jam. Check the tray, the rear path, the duplex unit, and the output area. Use a torch rather than sharp tools. If a scrap is wrapped around a roller, stop and seek service.

### 5. Check the tray and paper

Remove the paper stack and fan it gently. Discard damp, curled, torn, or previously printed sheets. Load paper below the maximum fill line and slide the guides so they touch the stack without bending it.

### 6. Close the covers and test

Close all covers firmly, plug the printer in, and power it on. The jam message should clear. Print a single test page, then a two-page document. If the printer jams again immediately, stop and check the path again rather than repeatedly retrying.

## Reduce repeat jams

- **Use the recommended paper weight** and keep paper sealed until use in humid rooms.
- **Do not mix paper sizes or weights** in the same tray.
- **Check the rear path** for duplex printing, which often needs a straight path for heavier paper.
- **Clean the pickup rollers** only as the manufacturer instructs; do not use household oils or solvents.
- **Replace worn rollers** if the printer consistently fails to pick up paper.

> [!WARNING]
> The fuser in a laser printer can be hot enough to burn even after the printer stops. Do not reach into the fuser area until it has cooled, and never use metal tools to fish out paper.

## Confirm the fix

Print a test page, a two-page document, and a duplex page if you use double-sided printing. Check that the sheets are not creased and that the jam message stays cleared. If jams return only with one paper type, stop using that paper rather than continuing to clear jams.
