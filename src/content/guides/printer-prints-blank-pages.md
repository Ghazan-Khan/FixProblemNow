## Separate printer output from computer output

Print the printer’s own test page or status page from its menu. This bypasses the computer, cable, and driver.

- **Printer test page is blank:** the problem is the cartridge, print head, or printer hardware.
- **Printer test page is fine:** the printer can mark paper, so the problem is the document, driver, or connection.

Do this before changing ink or reinstalling drivers; it prevents unnecessary work.

## Fix a blank printer test page

### 1. Check the cartridge seal and seating

Remove the cartridge and look for protective tape, a clip, or a seal that should have been removed. Reinstall it firmly until it clicks or seats as the manual describes. On a laser printer, do not touch the drum or the toner roller.

### 2. Check ink or toner level

Use the printer’s display or software to check the reported level. A level can be inaccurate after a refill, but an empty cartridge will not print. If a cartridge was refilled, the chip or vent may be the problem; use a genuine or reputable compatible cartridge.

### 3. Run a nozzle check

On an inkjet, use the printer’s maintenance menu to print a nozzle check pattern. Missing segments mean clogged nozzles. Run one cleaning cycle, print the pattern again, and repeat at most once or twice. Excessive cleaning wastes ink and can overheat the head.

### 4. Gently redistribute toner

On a laser printer, remove the cartridge and rock it gently side to side as the manufacturer instructs. Do not shake it aggressively or expose it to light for long. Reinstall and print a test page.

### 5. Check the paper

Confirm that the paper is loaded the right way up, especially photo paper. Check the paper type and size settings on the printer. A printer expecting photo paper may apply a different process, and a transparency or label setting can produce unexpected output.

## Fix blank pages from the computer

### 6. Check the print preview

Open the document and use the application’s print preview. Confirm the pages are not blank in the document itself. Check for white text, hidden layers, or a cover page setting.

### 7. Check driver and paper settings

In the print dialog, confirm the correct printer, paper size, orientation, and colour mode. Try the plain-paper default setting. If the document is a PDF, try printing from a different PDF reader to rule out the application.

### 8. Reinstall the driver

Remove the printer, restart the computer, and install the manufacturer’s driver for your exact model. A generic driver can cause formatting problems, including missing content on some printers.

### 9. Try another document and device

Print a plain text page from a different app. Then print from a phone or another computer. If other devices print correctly, the original computer’s driver or application is the cause.

## If pages are still blank

- **Replace the cartridge** with a known-good one, especially if the current cartridge is old, refilled, or third-party.
- **Check the printer’s error log** for a print-head or laser error.
- **Use the manufacturer’s diagnostic tool** if available.
- **Arrange service** for a failed print head, transfer belt, or laser unit.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not blow toner dust with compressed air; it spreads and can be inhaled. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for clearing a spill, and keep toner away from children and pets.

## Confirm the fix

Print the printer’s test page, then a colour test if applicable, then a normal document from the computer. Check that text and images appear across the whole page and that no bands or streaks remain. If the test page is fine but a specific document is blank, the problem is the document or application, not the printer.
