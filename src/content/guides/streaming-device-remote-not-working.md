## Test the remote and the device separately

Install the official phone app for the streaming device and connect it to the same network. Use the app to navigate.

- **The app works:** the device and network are fine, so the problem is the physical remote, its batteries, or its pairing.
- **The app does not work:** the device may be frozen or offline. Restart the device and check its network connection before replacing the remote.

## Fix the remote

### 1. Replace the batteries

Use fresh batteries of the correct type and orientation. Even a remote that lights up can have too little power to transmit reliably. Do not mix old and new batteries or different chemistries.

### 2. Clean the battery contacts

Remove the batteries and look for white or green corrosion. Clean the contacts with a dry cotton bud or a soft cloth. If corrosion has reached the circuit, the remote may be beyond repair.

### 3. Check line of sight for infrared remotes

Point the remote directly at the device or the TV’s IR receiver. Remove anything blocking the front of the device, and try from a shorter distance. If it only works up close, the batteries or the IR emitter are weak.

### 4. Re-pair a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi remote

Follow the manufacturer’s pairing procedure for your model. It usually involves holding a button combination while the device is on and near the remote. Keep the remote within a metre during pairing, and replace the batteries first.

### 5. Restart the streaming device

Unplug the device for a minute and power it back on. This clears the receiver state. After it starts, try the remote again before re-pairing.

### 6. Try the remote on another device

If you have another compatible streaming device, test the remote there. If it works, the original device’s receiver is the problem. If it fails, the remote is the problem.

## If the remote still will not work

- **Use the phone app** as a temporary or permanent alternative.
- **Check for a firmware update** on the device; a known remote bug may be fixed.
- **Look for a replacement remote** from the manufacturer or a reputable seller. Third-party remotes may lack features or pairing support.
- **Contact the manufacturer** if the device does not respond to any remote or app.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use a remote with leaking batteries. Battery fluid can burn skin and damage the device. Remove leaking cells with gloves and dispose of them according to local rules.

## Confirm the fix

Use the remote from your normal seating position. Test navigation, select, back, volume, and power. If it is a Bluetooth remote, confirm it works without pointing. Use the phone app once more to prove the device is responding normally.
