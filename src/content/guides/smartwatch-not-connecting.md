## Check the basics first

### 1. Charge the watch

Place it on the manufacturer’s charger and confirm it shows a charging indicator. A watch with very little charge may start pairing and then fail. Let it charge for at least 30 minutes before trying again.

### 2. Confirm compatibility

Check the watch’s supported phone models and operating-system versions. A watch app that requires a newer phone OS will not pair on an older phone. Some features also vary by phone brand.

### 3. Update the phone and the watch app

Install phone updates and the latest version of the official watch app. Open the app and grant the permissions it requests for Bluetooth, notifications, background activity, and health data. Denying background permission is a common reason sync stops later.

## Pair from the app

### 4. Remove the old pairing

On the phone, open Bluetooth settings and forget the watch. In the watch app, remove the existing watch if it is listed. On the watch, use the reset or unpair option if available. Removing only one side often leaves a half-paired state.

### 5. Restart Bluetooth and both devices

Turn Bluetooth off and on, then restart the phone. Restart the watch using its power menu. Keep both devices close together during pairing, and keep the app open.

### 6. Pair through the official app

Open the watch app and follow its Add device flow. Enter the pairing code shown on the watch if prompted. Do not pair only through the phone’s Bluetooth list; the app completes the setup and firmware checks.

### 7. Check battery optimisation

On Android, open the watch app’s battery settings and allow unrestricted background activity. Aggressive battery saving can stop sync and disconnect the watch when the screen is off. On iPhone, allow background app refresh for the watch app.

## If it still will not connect

- **Reset the watch’s network or pairing settings** if the manufacturer offers that option, then pair again.
- **Try another supported phone** to see whether the watch or the original phone is at fault.
- **Factory-reset the watch** after syncing any data you need. This removes accounts and settings.
- **Update the watch firmware** through the app once it connects, even briefly.
- **Check for water damage or a swollen battery** and stop if either is present.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not charge a watch with a swollen case or damaged contacts, and do not use a charger with the wrong voltage. A damaged lithium battery is a fire risk.

## Confirm the fix

Leave the watch connected for an hour and check that notifications and health data sync. Walk out of Bluetooth range and back to confirm it reconnects automatically. Restart the phone once and check that the watch reconnects without opening the app.
