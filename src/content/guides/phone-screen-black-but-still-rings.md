## Use the ringing as a clue

A phone that rings is not dead. The processor, modem, speaker, and battery are working well enough to receive a call. What has failed is the path that produces light: the display panel, the backlight, the display connector, or the software service that wakes the screen.

Before assuming the worst, confirm what still works:

- Call the phone from another device and listen for the ringtone.
- Press the power or side button and feel for a vibration.
- Plug in the charger and listen for the charging tone.
- Ask someone to send a message and see whether a notification sound plays.

## Start with the least invasive checks

### 1. Charge the phone properly

Connect the original or a known-good charger for at least 20 minutes. A deeply discharged battery can leave the display unpowered while the phone trickles charge. Check that the cable is fully seated and the port is free of lint.

### 2. Try a forced restart

This does not erase data. The exact buttons depend on the model:

- **iPhone with Face ID:** press volume up, press volume down, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.
- **iPhone with a Home button:** hold the side or top button and the Home button together.
- **Most Android phones:** hold the power button for 10–20 seconds, or use the power-and-volume combination documented by the manufacturer.

If the phone vibrates or makes a sound but the screen stays black, continue.

### 3. Shine a torch at the screen

Hold a bright torch at a low angle and look closely at the display. A faint image or a dim clock means the phone is drawing the screen but the backlight has failed. That pattern points to a display repair rather than a software fix.

### 4. Try to wake it with a known action

Ask someone to call again while you press the power button, or connect the charger and watch for a charging animation. If the phone reacts but the screen stays dark, the display path is the likely fault.

### 5. Check the proximity sensor and screen protector

A thick screen protector, dirt over the sensor, or a case that covers it can make the phone keep the screen off during calls. Remove the case and protector, clean the top of the screen, and test again.

## If those steps do not work

- **Connect to a computer.** If the phone is recognised, start a backup now. Do not update or reset the phone until important data is copied.
- **Use accessibility or assistant features.** Some phones can be controlled with a connected keyboard, voice assistant, or screen reader. This may let you answer a call or start a backup without seeing the screen.
- **Check for a known display issue.** Search your exact model and the symptom on the manufacturer’s support site. Some models have documented display faults or extended repair programmes.
- **Have the display inspected.** A repair technician can test the panel and connector. Ask for a quote before work begins, and confirm whether data will be preserved.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not press or pry a dark screen to “reseat” it, and do not charge a phone with a swollen battery. If the phone is hot, smells burning, or the case is bulging, disconnect power and keep it away from flammable material.

## Confirm the repair

After a repair or a successful restart, make a test call, lock and unlock the phone, and adjust the brightness to both extremes. Restart once more to confirm the fix survives a full boot. If the screen fails again when the phone is warm or after a drop, tell the technician; that pattern suggests a connector or panel fault rather than software.
